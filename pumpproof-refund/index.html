The Best Manager for your petrol pump, by Foundry Enterprises (SEQUEX)

Effective date: 27 November 2026

In one line: cancel anytime — future charges stop. Refunds apply where we charged wrongly or the service failed you, as set out below.

Cancelling

  • Monthly (auto-pay): cancel the mandate anytime from your UPI / banking app, or email us at krushna@sequex.co.in and we will cancel it for you. No further debits are made. Your plan stays active until the end of the month you have paid for.
  • Yearly: write to us to stop renewal. Your plan stays active until the end of the year you have paid for.
  • After a plan ends there is a 7-day grace period; after that, new entries pause and your data remains readable. You can restart anytime by buying a plan again.
  • The 30-day trial is free — there is nothing to cancel or refund.

When refunds apply

  • Duplicate or wrong charge (for example, charged twice for the same period): full refund of the wrong charge.
  • Payment taken but licence never activated due to a technical failure on our side: full refund, or activation — your choice.
  • Prolonged service failure: if the paid service is materially unusable because of a fault on our side for a continuous period of more than 7 days, a pro-rata refund for the affected period.
  • Other cases: fees for a billing period that has already started are not otherwise refundable, because the service is made available for the whole period from day one. This does not affect any rights you have under Indian consumer law.

How refunds are processed

  • Email krushna@sequex.co.in with your pump's name, the payment date and the Razorpay payment reference (it is on your payment receipt).
  • Approved refunds are made through Razorpay to the original payment method (UPI / card / bank account used for payment), within 5–7 working days of approval.
