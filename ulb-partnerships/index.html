How it works

Simple, documented and transparent — the same four steps for everyone.

1

Register / Share the requirement

Contractors register their company, experience and capacity. ULBs share the work they need executed.

2

Verification

We check track record, past work orders and capacity. Only credible partners are put forward.

3

Matching & introduction

The right partners are matched to the right work and introduced formally to the ULB.

4

Bid & execute — officially

Selection always happens through the ULB's official tender process. Our facilitation terms are agreed in writing, in advance.

Our commitment: We do not guarantee any tender award, and we do not influence any official decision. We widen participation, improve matching and support documentation — selection is always by the ULB, on merit, through its official process.
