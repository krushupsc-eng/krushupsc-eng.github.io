Our apps

Every app starts as a real problem faced by real people, gets tested with them in the field, and keeps improving from their feedback.

Nond — Work Evidence App

For contractors & site teams

Capture Before, During and After photos with GPS and time stamps, and turn them into print-ready A4 evidence sheets for billing. Works fully offline — no signal needed on site.

Coming soon on Google Play

PumpProof

For petrol pump owners & staff

The complete petrol pump manager: shift closing with photo proof, dip and density readings, credit ledger with WhatsApp reminders, tanker shortage checks and DSR reports — designed with real pump owners.

In field pilot
📍

GPS Map Camera

For field work, surveys & inspections

A camera that stamps every photo with its GPS location, address, date and time — proof-grade photos for field documentation, surveys and site inspections.

Coming soon on Google Play
🏏

GullyCaptain

Scoring and management for gully and box cricket: start a match in under a minute, score ball by ball fully offline, and run tournaments with points tables and player stats.

About

Foundry Enterprises

SEQUEX is the product brand of Foundry Enterprises, an MSME-registered firm from Nevasa, Ahilyanagar, Maharashtra, founded in February 2026.

We build software the way the field teaches us: find a real problem, build the simplest thing that solves it, put it in real users' hands, and let their feedback shape the next version. Our apps are in daily use — from a one-lakh-household municipal survey to a working petrol pump.
