About

Foundry Enterprises

SEQUEX is the brand of Foundry Enterprises, an MSME-registered firm from Nevasa, Ahilyanagar, Maharashtra, founded in February 2026. Our main business is municipal projects and ULB partnerships; our apps grow out of that same field work.
