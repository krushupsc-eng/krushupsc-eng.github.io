🏏 GullyCaptain — Privacy Policy

The captain's app for gully cricket, by Foundry Enterprises (SEQUEX)

Effective date: 17 November 2026

In one line: GullyCaptain collects no data at all.

The app has no internet permission, no account or sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Everything you create in the app stays on your own phone.

What data we collect

None. We do not collect, receive, store on any server, sell, or share any personal or usage data. There is no server for this app — it works completely offline.

Data stored on your device

The teams, player names, matches, tournaments, expense records and polls you create are stored only in the app's private storage on your device, so the app can show them to you again. This data:

  • never leaves your device through the app;
  • is not backed up by the app to any cloud service (Android backup is disabled for this app);
  • is deleted permanently when you delete it in the app or uninstall the app.

Permissions

GullyCaptain requests no device permissions — no camera, no location, no contacts, no storage, no internet.

Sharing

The only way anything leaves the app is when you tap a Share button and choose an app yourself (for example WhatsApp) to send a scorecard, points table, poll or payment reminder. What you send is then handled by that app under its own privacy policy. We receive nothing.

Children

GullyCaptain is a general-audience sports scorekeeping tool. Because the app collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children either.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app ever changes how data is handled, this page will be updated first and the change will be described here with a new effective date.
