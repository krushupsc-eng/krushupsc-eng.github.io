i About the Role

Green India Service Provider Pvt. Ltd. delivers IEC (Information, Education & Communication), capacity building and solid waste management services to Urban Local Bodies (ULBs) under Swachh Bharat Mission – Urban 2.0.

We are hiring an IEC Expert who will be based in a Tier-2 city in Maharashtra and will handle the complete IEC work for 2–3 ULBs assigned by the company at the same time — planning, field campaigns, team handling and all Swachh Survekshan / GFC documentation for those ULBs.

1 Roles & Responsibilities

Planning & Strategy

  • Prepare monthly / quarterly / annual IEC & BCC action plans and activity calendars aligned with SBM-U 2.0 objectives, the Swachh Survekshan toolkit and MoHUA guidelines; get them approved by the ULB.
  • Prioritise high-impact wards, bulk waste generators, schools, RWAs and market areas.

Campaigns & Community Engagement

  • Lead awareness campaigns on source segregation (wet, dry, domestic hazardous, sanitary waste), plastic ban & Plastic Waste Management Rules, RRR (Reduce–Reuse–Recycle), cleanliness drives, Zero Waste Events and monthly SBM thematic campaigns.
  • Door-to-door outreach, school awareness sessions and student ambassador activities, RWA / market / hospital engagement, street plays, competitions and cleanliness drives with residents, NGOs, CBOs and volunteers.
  • Drive citizen participation in RRR centres and community-level behaviour change activities.

IEC Material, Media & Social Media

  • Design and disseminate IEC material — posters, pamphlets, brochures and wall paintings.
  • Prepare public service announcements for local media / radio and content for ULB social media handles; document and publish success stories.

Training, Team & Coordination

  • Conduct trainings and workshops for sanitation workers, community groups, schools, RWAs and institutions.
  • Lead, allocate work to and monitor IEC field teams, coordinators and volunteers across the assigned ULBs.
  • Coordinate with ULB officials, NGOs, CBOs and other stakeholders for joint activities.

Documentation, MIS & Reporting

  • Maintain photo / video / attendance evidence for every activity conducted.
  • Prepare progress reports, completion reports and all documentation required for Swachh Survekshan and Garbage Free City (GFC) assessments.
  • Report to the ULB and the company; use surveys and citizen feedback to improve campaigns.

2 Eligibility & Skills

  • Post Graduation in Social Work (MSW), Mass Communication / Journalism, Environmental Studies / Science, Public Health, Urban Planning, Development Studies or a related field.
  • 3–5 years of relevant IEC / BCC, community mobilisation, urban sanitation or SBM-U experience; experience working with ULBs / Municipal Corporations preferred.
  • Fluency in Marathi, Hindi and English; strong report writing and documentation skills.
  • Willingness for extensive field work and travel between the assigned ULBs.
