Questions pump owners ask us

Straight answers, no fine print.

Does PumpProof work without internet?

Yes — fully. Daily work (shift close, credit, dips) needs no internet at all. When any phone gets internet, the data syncs in the background, so the owner sees the pump's position from anywhere. The dispenser meter is always the final truth; work never waits for a network.

How many phones and staff can use it?

As many as your pump needs. The price is per pump, not per user — the owner, managers and all attendants are included, each with their own login and the powers you choose for them.

What happens when the free trial ends?

You get a 7-day grace period after the trial. If no plan is active after that, new entries pause — but your old data stays readable, always. Your data is never held hostage.

What exactly does the launch price mean?

The standard monthly price is ₹749. During the launch window (the first 6 months from public launch), new pumps start at ₹499/month. Pumps that join in this window keep ₹499 for their first 12 months, then move to the standard ₹749.

How do I pay, and can I stop?

On this page, via Razorpay — UPI, cards or netbanking. The monthly plan runs on an auto-pay mandate you approve once; you can cancel it anytime from your UPI app or by writing to us, and future charges stop. See our refund & cancellation policy.

Is my pump's data private?

Yes. Your data belongs to your pump. It is stored on your team's phones and in our secure cloud so your phones stay in sync. We do not sell data, show ads, or track you. Read the privacy policy.
