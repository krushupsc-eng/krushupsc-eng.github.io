⛽ PumpProof — Privacy Policy

The Best Manager for your petrol pump, by Foundry Enterprises (SEQUEX)

Effective date: 27 November 2026

In one line: PumpProof handles your pump's business data so your team can work together — we never sell it, show ads, or track you.

PumpProof is a business app for petrol pump owners, managers and attendants. This policy explains what data the app handles, where it is stored, and the choices you have.

Who we are

Foundry Enterprises (brand: SEQUEX), Nevasa, Ahilyanagar, Maharashtra, India. Contact: krushna@sequex.co.in.

Data the app handles

  • Account details. The pump owner signs in with a Google account (name and email). Staff logins are created by the owner and use the staff member's name, mobile number and role (Manager / Attendant).
  • Your pump's business records. What your team enters in the app: shift readings and closings, dip and density records, stock and tanker receipts, credit (udhaar) entries — including credit customers' names, mobile numbers, vehicle numbers and balances — lubricant sales, expenses, attendance, and the pump's settings.
  • Photos. Proof photos your team takes in the app (meter readings, credit slips, invoices, dips). Photos are taken only when a user taps the photo button.
  • Login security data. To protect accounts, we record the device model and login times for each login. The pump owner can see the list of devices signed in to the pump's accounts and can revoke a device.

Where the data lives

  • On your team's phones, in the app's private storage (photos are not saved to the phone gallery).
  • When signed in, records sync to our secure cloud database (hosted on Supabase) so the pump's phones stay in step and the owner can view the pump remotely. Photos sync on Wi-Fi.
  • Access is controlled per pump: only members of your pump can see your pump's data.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, no sale of data, no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs in the app.
  • We do not read your data for marketing. We access account data only to run the service, provide support you ask for, or meet a legal obligation.

Payments

Plans are bought on our website (sequex.co.in/pumpproof) and processed by Razorpay. Your card / UPI details go directly to Razorpay — we never receive or store them. We keep the payment record (amount, plan, pump, date) to activate and manage your licence.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Camera — to take proof photos inside the app.
  • Internet — for sign-in and sync. The app works fully offline; sync happens when a connection is available.

Sharing

We share data only with the service providers needed to run PumpProof (cloud hosting and payment processing), and only the minimum each needs. We may disclose data if required by law.

Your choices and account deletion

  • The pump owner controls staff logins: he can create them, change PINs, and switch any login off at any time.
  • Delete your account in the app: More → Account → “Account Delete”. If you are the sole owner of a pump, that pump's cloud data is deleted with the account. Data on phones is removed by uninstalling the app.
  • Delete via the web: email krushna@sequex.co.in with the subject “Delete my PumpProof account” from your registered email / phone number, and we will delete your account and confirm by email.
  • Business records a pump keeps about its own customers (for example credit balances) are the pump's records; the pump owner manages and can delete them in the app.

Children

PumpProof is a business tool for adults who run or work at petrol pumps. It is not directed at children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will update this page and note the new effective date.
